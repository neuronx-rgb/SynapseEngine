Salon Appointment Booking System
==================================

The system should be fast and intuitive for both staff and customers. Users can create
accounts and log in to book appointments. The booking process should be secure.

1. Customers can book, reschedule, or cancel appointments as needed.
2. The system must handle a reasonable number of concurrent users efficiently.
3. Staff can manage their availability and view upcoming appointments.
4. Customers can upload files such as reference photos for their appointment.
5. The system should send reminders to customers at an appropriate time before appointments.
6. Payment can be made online or in-person, and the system should handle it appropriately.
7. The admin can generate reports quickly.
8. Customer data should be stored securely and kept for as long as necessary.
9. The system must be scalable to support future growth.
10. New features should be easy to add without breaking existing functionality.
11. Users and/or staff can leave reviews after appointments.
12. The system must not be unavailable during business hours.
