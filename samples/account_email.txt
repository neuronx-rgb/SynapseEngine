User Account & Email Management Requirements
============================================

1. Every user account must have a globally unique email address in the system.
2. Email addresses must be validated using standard RFC 5322 format rules.
3. Family members can share a single email address across multiple accounts.
4. Users must verify their email address within 48 hours of registration.
5. A user can change their email address up to 3 times per calendar year.
6. The system must send password reset links to the user's registered email.
7. Accounts inactive for more than 12 months must be automatically deactivated.
8. Administrators can manually assign or reassign email addresses to any account.
9. The system must log all email change events with timestamp and IP address.
10. Users can set a secondary email address for notifications as needed.
11. Email addresses must not be reused for 6 months after account deletion.
12. The system should send users weekly digest emails if they opt in.
